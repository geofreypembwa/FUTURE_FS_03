About Us

Food Convenience at UDSM Main Campus

Campus Cafeteria is a convenient food spot located at UDSM Main Campus, Dar es Salaam, serving students, staff, visitors, and the wider campus community.

We offer a wide range of meals, snacks, fresh juices, dairy products, soft drinks, and breakfast options at affordable prices. Our goal is to provide tasty food, quick service, and a comfortable place where people can eat, relax, and enjoy a variety of choices throughout the day.

With dine-in, takeaway, and delivery options available, Campus Cafeteria helps make food access easier and more enjoyable for everyone on campus.
